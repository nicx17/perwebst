Two-digit BCD counter, 00 → 99

Two 74LS90 decade counters in BCD mode count pulses from an NE555 one-shot. Two CD4513 decoders turn each 4-bit BCD value into segments on a pair of 5161AS displays. Press the COUNT button or any button on an IR remote and the number goes up by one. RESET returns it to 00. Everything runs on 5 V from USB-C, on one 80 × 55 mm FR2 perfboard.

Perfboard layout · 80 × 55 mm

Rows A–T × columns 1–30 on the FR2 board, the same grid as the LED chaser. Bare wire along row B is the +5 V rail and along row S the ground rail. Most signal wiring is insulated wire on the solder side. Flip the view to see it, drawn mirrored the way you see the board turned over. Hover a row in the wire table to highlight that wire.

Decoder part: use the CD4513BE (DIP-18). Display check: 5161AS = common cathode, which is what the CD4513 needs. A 5161BS won't light. Supply: 5 V only, from the same kind of USB-C breakout as the chaser. Never use a higher supply: the 74LS90s are rated 7 V absolute maximum.

Build order

  1. Rails. On the solder side, lay bare tinned wire along row B and row S (columns 1–30). Solder it every few holes.
  2. Bare links. Switch to the solder view and make every silver line: bare wire for the long ones, a solder bridge for neighbouring pads. The +5 V links are column 1 (B–K), row K (1–11), column 11 (K–R) and row P (12–29). Several run under the sockets, so do them before fitting the sockets.
  3. C1 first. It's the SMD electrolytic and lives under U1 on the solder side: + on E11, − (striped end) on G11.
  4. Sockets. U1 DIP-8 with pin 1 at D9, notch toward row A. U3 and U2 DIP-14 with pin 1 at L1 and L6, notch toward row A. U4 and U5 DIP-18 lying sideways with pin 1 at R12 and R22, notch toward column 1.
  5. Resistors. Fit R2 flat, R1/R3/R26 and the eight 4.7 kΩ pull-ups standing up, then the fourteen 330 Ω segment resistors flat in columns 13–19 and 23–29 (rows J–N).
  6. Capacitors. C2 10 nF, C3, C4, C5 and C7 100 nF ceramics. C6 10 µF: + in G1, stripe in G2.
  7. Buttons and displays. COUNT at E4/E7/G4/G7 and RESET at C27/C30/E27/E30, each wired on its diagonal legs. Displays with pin 1 at I15 and I21: the decimal point sits bottom-right.
  8. Solder-side wiring. Work through the wire table group by group: power and control first, then BCD, then segments. Tick each one off.
  9. Off-board. USB-C breakout VBUS → B1, GND → S1, with 5.1 kΩ from CC1 and CC2 to GND on the breakout. IR module + → C5, OUT → C7, − → G3. Match the silk on your module, because pin orders differ.
  10. Test before the chips. No beep between rails B and S. Power up, then check 5 V at U1-8, U2-5, U3-5, U4-18 and U5-18 sockets and at U4-3/4 (LT/BI). Power off, insert the chips, power on: you should see 00.
