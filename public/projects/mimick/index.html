Everything in one app

Backup and your whole library, natively.

Mimick started as a folder-sync daemon. It's now a complete Immich client — browse, edit, search, map and share from a GTK4 window while uploads keep running in the background.

Automatic folder backup

Watch any folder and upload new photos and videos as they land — to your library, an album, or an album named after the folder. Upload Only, Download Only or Full sync, per folder.

Reliable on any network

Failed uploads are saved and retried, Mimick switches between your home and remote server addresses, and it can pause on metered networks, on battery, or in quiet hours.

Your whole Immich library

A Photos timeline with a date scrubber, albums, Explore, People, Tags, Folders, Favorites, Archive and Trash. Rate, archive, re-date or re-place many photos at once.

Memories, stacks & live photos

"On this day" memories above Photos, bursts and RAW+JPEG pairs grouped into stacks, and live and motion photos that play their clip right in the viewer.

A viewer for big photos

Swipe between photos, zoom to 2000% of the real pixels as full resolution streams in, play hardware-decoded video, and run slideshows. RAW previews included.

Non-destructive editing

Crop, rotate and straighten; adjust light and colour; apply filters; redact faces or text. Crops can be reverted, and other edits save as a copy that keeps the original's metadata.

Tag the people

Name, move or remove the faces Immich found, draw a box around ones it missed, merge duplicates, and point at a face to see who it is.

Find any photo

Smart search, search by the text in a photo (OCR) and copy it, find similar photos, filter by camera or place, and clear out look-alikes on the Duplicates page.

Your photos on a map

See where your photos were taken, search any place or address, and pick Immich's style, VersaTiles, satellite imagery or OpenStreetMap.

Share and comment

Shared albums with Viewer and Editor roles, likes and comments, public links with expiry and password, and partner sharing.

Made for the Linux desktop

Tray icon, start on login, drag and drop, Open With Mimick from your file manager, and keyboard shortcuts for nearly everything (Ctrl+?).

Works on Linux phones

The layout adapts down to 360 px with a bottom navigation bar. Swipe, pinch to zoom and long-press for menus.

Background sync

Drop a folder. Walk away.

Point Mimick at your camera roll, screenshots or exports. New photos and videos upload as they appear, and the Status page shows exactly where things stand.

  • Upload Only, Download Only, or Full sync, set per folder
  • SHA-1 checksums skip anything already on the server
  • Switches between LAN and WAN addresses on its own
  • Pauses on metered networks, on battery, or in quiet hours
  • Failed uploads are kept, retried, and listed in the Queue Inspector
Viewer

Built for big photos.

Even 200-megapixel photos open quickly. Zoom to 2000% of the real pixels as full resolution loads, or play hardware-decoded video streamed straight from Immich.

  • Details pane with rating, people, camera, lens and a mini map
  • Live and motion photos play their clip in place
  • Bursts and RAW+JPEG pairs grouped into stacks
  • Slideshows with crossfade, shuffle and repeat
  • JPEG XL, HEIF, AVIF, JPEG 2000, PSD and RAW
Editor

Edit without touching the original.

Crops are kept in Immich and can be reverted. Everything else saves as a new copy, stacked with the original and carrying its metadata.

  • Crop, rotate, flip and straighten with a horizon wheel
  • Ten adjustments, from exposure and highlights to warmth and vignette
  • Nine filters, previewed on your own photo
  • Redact faces, plates or text with pixelate, blur, frosted glass or a bar
Map

Your photos, on a map.

See where every photo was taken, from country-sized clusters down to a single street, and open the photos in any area beside the map.

  • Search any place or address, powered by Photon
  • Immich's style, a dozen VersaTiles styles, satellite, or OpenStreetMap
  • Light and dark variants that follow the app
  • Set or fix the location of one photo or many at once
Linux phones

  • Swipe, pinch to zoom, and long-press for menus

Under the hood

The quiet, useful details.

The bits that make Mimick a daemon you forget you have running — until it’s the thing that saved your library.

01

Atomic file monitoring

Mimick waits for write locks to release and file sizes to settle before queuing — so a still-encoding video never uploads half-finished.

02

Live-apply settings

Upload workers, quiet hours, folder rules and album targets take effect the moment you change them. No restart, no daemon reload.

03

Catch-up, not crawl

A sharded local sync index remembers what's already on the server and survives clearing the cache. Startup scans run in parallel and only look at what changed.

04

Streams, doesn't hoard

Uploads stream from disk with the same memory whatever the file size, and in-progress uploads finish or are saved for retry when you quit.

05

Gives memory back

The timeline loads only the months you scroll to, thumbnails decode at tile size, a Memory Limit caps the cache, and closing the library releases it.

06

One-click diagnostics

Export a redacted support bundle — summary.txt, config, status, retries and sync index — straight from the Status page.

07

Selected-folder sandbox

The Flatpak asks the desktop portal for only the folders you pick — never your whole ~. API keys live in the system keyring.

08

Signed & verifiable

The Flatpak repository is signed, releases ship with SHA256SUMS, and every commit runs through CodeQL, SonarCloud, lint, test and audit checks.

09

Ready for your language

The interface is translatable with gettext, so anyone can add a language — with a progress chart in the README to show how far each one has come.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Mimick an official Immich app?

No. Mimick is a free, open-source community project, licensed under the GPL v3 or later. It isn't made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Immich team.

How do I back up a folder to Immich automatically?

Install Mimick, enter your server address and an Immich API key, and add the folder under Watch Folders. New photos and videos upload in the background from then on.

Will Mimick change or delete my local files?

Not by default. Folders start as Upload Only, which never changes local files. Download Only and Full sync add your album's photos to the folder, and two-way deletion sync is opt-in.

Which Immich versions are supported?

Immich v3.0.0 and later.

Where is my API key stored?

In your desktop's keyring through the Secret Service (GNOME Keyring, KWallet). If no keyring is running, Mimick falls back to a key file instead of hanging at startup.

Does it run on Linux phones?

Yes. The layout adapts down to 360 pixels wide with a bottom navigation bar, and you can swipe, pinch to zoom and long-press for menus. AppImages are built for aarch64 as well as x86_64.
